## Direction contract

THESIS: Lumina is a public poster series on the street, not an NGO template. Every section is the next poster rolling into a bus-shelter case. It refuses the hero-photo-plus-card-grid foundation page.

OWN-WORLD: flat ink fields in roos, wijn, blad and perzik with white. Marigold exists only for the primary action. One bold variable grotesque at poster scale, a highly legible body face. Photographs live inside one petal shape taken from the logo leaf. Thin aluminium-like frame around every poster, perforated tear-off strips, one rigid twelve-column grid.

STORY: the visitor sees women dancing and the name at street scale, understands there is a place for her, and tears off a strip: meedoen, iets betekenen or samenwerking. She scrolls through the four programmes, sees small acts gather into one bloom, learns the steps, then contacts Lumina.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-frame roos poster. Thin white header. "Lumina Collective" in huge type left and bleeding wide, the dancers in a giant petal on the right overlapping the type, claim in the corner, intro and marigold Doe mee lower left, three tear-off strips across the bottom edge.

FORM: Dutch public poster series in a bus shelter, position 6 on the ordered list, seed:7a43fe3de2dfda40. Signature interactions: poster roll on scroll, and photo petals gathering into a bloom beside the policy-plan quote. Motion grammar: roll, bloom of type weight on arrival, tear of a strip, one step row showing the current poster.

Raises: marigold reserved for the action (Creator Hardware Bench). One running step row always saying where you are (drum machine step row). Type weight tracks arrival (chromatophore skin). One rigid grid (ANSI BBS). Fixed label band on each programme with audience and next step (pickling brine calendar).
