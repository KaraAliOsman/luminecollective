# Photography Register

Current local photography is in `public/images/*.webp`. Source files are delivered
as compressed WebP by the Pexels image CDN, without AI modification or colour
grading. The originals remain available through the credited source pages.

These are illustrative stock scenes, not records of Lumina activities or portraits
of its participants. The public `/fotografie` page makes this distinction explicit.
The [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permits the intended website use.
The supplied foundation logo and policy PDF are not stock assets.
